Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become a member?

You can sign up online through our website or visit us in person. Basic membership is free, and we also offer premium plans with additional benefits.

How many books can I borrow at once?

This depends on your membership level. Basic members can borrow up to 3 books, Standard members up to 5 books, and Premium members have unlimited borrowing.

What if I return a book late?

Late returns may incur a small daily fee. However, you can renew your books online before the due date to avoid any charges.

Do you offer digital books?

Yes! Standard and Premium members have access to our extensive collection of e-books and audiobooks available 24/7 through our online platform.

Can I reserve a book that's currently checked out?

Absolutely! You can place a hold on any book through our online catalog, and we'll notify you when it becomes available.

Is there parking available?

Yes, we have free parking available for all library visitors. There's also convenient public transportation access nearby.
